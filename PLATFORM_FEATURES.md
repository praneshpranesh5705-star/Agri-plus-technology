# Agri Nature AI — Complete Platform Scope

## Positioning
**Ancient Wisdom × Modern Science × AI**

This repository is designed as an **Agricultural Knowledge + Engineering + Innovation Ecosystem**, not a static agriculture information website.

## Included product layers

1. **A–Z agriculture knowledge** — crops, soil, water, seeds, nutrients, crop protection, harvesting, post-harvest, storage, marketing, economics and climate resilience.
2. **Agricultural engineering** — tractor and implements, tillage, sowing/planting, transplanting, irrigation, sprayers, harvesters, threshers, processing, renewable energy and smart machinery.
3. **Machine decision support** — operation + crop + soil/land condition + farm size + capacity + power/HP + safety + maintenance + source; verified price/manufacturer fields are reserved for sourced records.
4. **Fertilizer database schema** — name, brand, manufacturer, origin, composition, nutrient analysis, crop/context, label/source, package, price/date and precautions. No invented site-specific doses.
5. **Seed/variety database schema** — crop, variety, developer/company, origin, duration, season, soil, irrigation, traits, region, seed rate, spacing, sowing and harvest, with source.
6. **Farm Vision AI** — land photo + location + area + previous crop + water + soil-test data + desired crop + budget → structured decision-support plan. Photos are observations, not laboratory soil tests.
7. **Design a machine for my farm** — problem → constraints → sensors → controller → mechanics → power → software → BOM → safety → testing → environmental impact.
8. **Tamil traditional agriculture** — traditional seeds, tools, water systems, seasonal knowledge, indigenous livestock and ecological practices, with evidence labels and Tamil/English support.
9. **Traditional → science → technology bridge** — documented practice → scientific explanation → engineering improvement → measurement.
10. **Nature protection** — soil, water, biodiversity, pollinators, agroforestry, climate resilience, renewable energy, waste reduction and circular farming.
11. **Integrated/circular farming** — crop + livestock + fish + trees + pollinators + resource recycling.
12. **Water intelligence** — rain/catchment/tank/canal/field/groundwater concepts, sensors, IoT, GIS and automation.
13. **Precision agriculture** — GPS/GNSS, GIS, satellite/drone imagery, NDVI concepts, sensor networks, IoT, variable-rate concepts and decision support.
14. **Robotics lab** — weed monitoring, soil monitoring, crop scouting, seeding, spraying, harvest assistance, obstacle avoidance, irrigation and autonomous navigation concepts.
15. **Green technology** — solar irrigation, biogas, biomass, electric machinery, efficient pumps, waste-to-value and renewable energy.
16. **AI modes** — Farmer, Student, Engineering, Heritage, Nature, Research, Innovation and Farm Vision.
17. **Student Innovation Lab** — problem → concept → sensors → controller → mechanical design → software → cost → prototype → testing → improvement → commercialization.
18. **Learning tracks** — Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Engineering, AI, Research, Traditional and Innovation.
19. **Public awareness** — WHAT → WHY → WHERE → WHEN → HOW → MATERIALS → COST → SAFETY → ENVIRONMENT → TRADITION → MODERN TECHNOLOGY → FUTURE.
20. **Farm 2040/2050 roadmap** — autonomous machines, digital twins, smart greenhouses, AI farm managers, autonomous drones, sensor irrigation, climate-resilient crops, renewable farms, robotics, advanced breeding and automated post-harvest.
21. **Source-first architecture** — every important record should carry source organisation, document/page, URL, publication/update date, access/check date and evidence level.
22. **Live-data connectors planned** — weather, eNAM/market prices, government schemes/subsidies, machinery records, seed/fertilizer/product records, maps, remote sensing, agriculture news and ESP32/IoT.

## AgriBot AI rover integration

The platform includes the concept:

`SENSORS + CAMERA → ESP32/CONTROLLER → AI/DECISION LOGIC → ROVER MOVEMENT → FIELD MONITORING → MOBILE APP → SOIL/CROP DATA → ALERTS → FARMER ACTION`

Prototype components can include soil-moisture, temperature/humidity, camera/vision, GPS/IMU, ultrasonic obstacle sensing, motors, motor driver, rechargeable battery, pump, relay/solenoid and wireless connectivity. A future design can move the soil probe away from the rover body, measure moisture and irrigate only after the measurement/decision step.

## Evidence policy

- **Official/verified:** government, ICAR, TNAU, agricultural universities, official manufacturer documentation.
- **Research evidence:** peer-reviewed research and recognised research institutions.
- **Traditional/documented practice:** recorded Indigenous Technical Knowledge or traditional ecological knowledge.
- **Industry information:** manufacturer or reputable market data, clearly dated.
- **Unverified claim:** must not be presented as established fact.

## Safety policy

The platform is decision support. Fertilizer/pesticide quantities, machinery operation, electrical systems, drones, pumps and mechanical systems require current label/manual guidance and appropriate professional/local advice. The AI must never invent a dose, certification, price, manufacturer specification or safety claim.

## Future backend

The static frontend is structured so it can later connect to APIs/database services for live weather, market prices, product records, authentication/admin, AI vision, ESP32 telemetry, maps/GIS and source-managed knowledge records.
